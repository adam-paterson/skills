# Fit Screen Decision Log

Use this log after product truth, user jobs, and research patterns are mapped. Its purpose is to show why the shortlist survived and why other candidates were deferred or rejected.

Keep the log bounded: record the most decision-relevant candidates, not every brainstormed idea. A typical discovery report should include 3-6 rejected or deferred items unless the user asks for a deeper appendix.

## Decision Log

| Candidate | Source or user job | Disposition | Reason | Evidence grade | Next move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `<opportunity or candidate>` | Product truth / user job / direct competitor / adjacent offering / semantic analogue / platform expectation / commercial cue | Shortlist / Research-first / Defer / Reject / Already present | `<fit-screen reason>` | Observed / Researched / Inferred / Unverified | Ship / Prototype / Research / Defer / Reject |

## Dispositions

- **Shortlist:** Strong enough to become a dossier now.
- **Research-first:** Potentially valuable, but a material assumption must be validated before it deserves a dossier or delivery work.
- **Defer:** Plausible fit, but not now because of timing, effort, sequencing, dependency, or weaker pain.
- **Reject:** Does not fit the product, user job, strategy, evidence, or cost/benefit bar.
- **Already present:** The capability, workflow, or service layer already exists in inspected product truth. Mention only if it explains why a tempting idea is not shortlisted.

## Fit-Screen Rules

Reject or defer candidates when any of these apply:

- **Already present:** The target product or service already solves the job well enough.
- **Off-position:** The idea pulls the product toward a different audience, category, promise, or operating model.
- **Too generic:** The idea is broadly nice-to-have but not connected to a specific user job, friction point, or strategic outcome.
- **Too costly for likely benefit:** The candidate requires high implementation, operational, compliance, support, or maintenance cost without proportionate leverage.
- **Unsupported by a user job:** The candidate is attractive as a feature but lacks a clear user, trigger, desired outcome, and friction point.
- **Copycat-only:** The main reason to build it is that another organization has it; the underlying pattern has not been translated into this product's context.
- **Weak evidence:** The idea depends on unverified market, pricing, competitor, or customer claims and should be researched before it becomes a recommendation.
- **Wrong commercial boundary:** The opportunity would create upgrade pressure, support burden, trust risk, or service expectations that do not fit the value model.

## Completion Criteria

The fit screen is complete when:

- Every shortlisted opportunity has survived an explicit fit reason.
- Important rejected or deferred candidates have a concise reason.
- Already-present candidates are removed from the shortlist.
- Research-first items have a validation next move, not a vague “maybe later.”
- The final report includes enough rejected/deferred context to explain judgment without becoming an idea dump.
