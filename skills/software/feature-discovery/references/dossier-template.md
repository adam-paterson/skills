# Opportunity Dossier Template

Use this template for each shortlisted opportunity. Keep each dossier concise, but do not omit a field unless it is clearly not applicable.

## <Opportunity name>

**Concept:** What the opportunity is, using the domain adapter's native feature vocabulary.

**Why now:** The product truth, user job, market pattern, constraint, or timing signal that makes this worth considering now.

**User and product benefit:** The progress this creates for users and the strategic benefit for the product or service.

**Primary users and use cases:** Who uses it, when they encounter it, and what job it helps complete.

**Commercial canvas:**

- **Value path:** Acquisition, activation, retention, expansion, direct revenue, cost-to-serve reduction, trust, compliance, community health, network growth, or another strategic value path.
- **Buyer / payer / user:** Who uses it, who pays or approves, and whether those are different people.
- **Packaging mechanism:** Core inclusion, freemium limit, trial, credit, quota, tier, usage meter, transaction fee, marketplace take-rate, revenue share, add-on, premium integration, automation pack, template pack, analytics module, admin/control module, white-label option, bundle, service package, membership, implementation, training, support, managed offering, sponsorship, advertising, partner/channel funding, lead generation, ecosystem incentive, or non-revenue lever.
- **Value metric or limit:** The unit that maps value to packaging, such as seats, usage, volume, transactions, storage, projects, automations, integrations, reporting depth, compliance scope, support level, service hours, templates, audience reach, or marketplace activity.
- **Cost-to-serve impact:** Whether this increases operational/support burden, reduces manual work, changes infrastructure cost, or shifts risk.
- **Validation question:** The commercial assumption that must be true for this opportunity to make sense.

**Evidence:** Label each supporting point with an evidence grade: **Observed** for inspected source material, **Researched** for current cited external research, **Inferred** for plausible but unproven reasoning, or **Unverified** for claims that need validation before being treated as true.

**Risks and tradeoffs:** Cost, complexity, positioning risk, user confusion, operational burden, cannibalization, support load, or reasons the opportunity may not fit.

**Smallest validation step:** The cheapest next test: user interview question, fake door, landing-page test, prototype, concierge/manual version, sales conversation, analytics check, or implementation spike.
